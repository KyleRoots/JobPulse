How we review applications

We use automated tools, including Scout Screening (an AI-assisted review system), to help our recruiters evaluate applications for job fit. This may include analysis of your resume, work history, skills, and the information you submit on this form.

A recruiter makes all final decisions. AI recommendations do not automatically accept or reject applicants.
